THE COIN GAME - MULTIPLAYER
===========================
version 0.9.8  (public beta)

Play The Coin Game with friends. You share an island, take turns on the
machines, watch each other play, and keep your own money.


INSTALL
-------

Three steps. Ten minutes the first time, about thirty seconds every time
after that.


STEP 1 - find your game folder

In Steam, right-click The Coin Game, then Manage, then Browse local files.
A window opens on the folder with TheCoinGame.exe in it. Leave it open,
you need it twice.


STEP 2 - install MelonLoader

MelonLoader is what lets mods run at all. It is not made by me. Get it from
the official site:

  https://melonloader.net

Download MelonLoader.Installer.exe and run it.

  - Click SELECT and point it at TheCoinGame.exe, in the folder from step 1
  - Version:  v0.6.6      (not "Latest" - pick 0.6.6 from the list)
  - Tick x64. Leave everything else alone.
  - Click INSTALL

Now launch the game once from Steam and let it reach the main menu. The
first start is slow - MelonLoader is building its cache and can sit on a
black screen for a minute or two. That is normal and only happens once.

Quit the game when you see the menu.


STEP 3 - drop the mod in

Open the zip you downloaded. Inside is a folder called Mods.

Drag that Mods folder into your game folder from step 1, next to
TheCoinGame.exe. If Windows asks about merging, say yes - MelonLoader made
a Mods folder in step 2 and you are adding a file to it.

When you are done you should have:

  <game folder>\Mods\TcgMultiplayer.dll

If you have a Mods folder inside another Mods folder, drag it one level up.
That is the only thing that usually goes wrong here.

Launch from Steam as normal. Press F9. If the panel opens, you are done.


Everyone playing needs to do all three steps, and everyone needs their own
copy of the game.

IF YOU ALREADY RUN OTHER COIN GAME MODS: most of them use BepInEx, and this
one uses MelonLoader. Two mod loaders in one game folder usually means the
game starts with no mods at all and no hint why. Pick one.


PLAYING
-------

BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: load your save, then press F10.

That runs a self-check - the wire format, malformed packets, machine ids,
the wallet guard, the save backup, and that visiting someone else's island
gives your own progression back. It takes a couple of seconds. Press "Copy
result" and send it over.

It does not test Steam actually delivering packets between two machines.
That's what we're all here to find out.

Press F9 to open the panel.

  Host           starts a session on your island
  Invite friend  opens the Steam overlay to invite someone
  Leave          ends your session

Your friend can also join from Steam directly - right-click you in the
friends list, Join Game.

Everyone must be running the same version of the mod. The mod checks and
will refuse to connect if you aren't, telling you which version each of you
has. You should also be on the same version of the game; it'll warn you
about that too.


WHAT'S SHARED AND WHAT ISN'T
----------------------------

Shared:      the island. Area unlocks, doors, and vehicles bought.
             Machine occupancy - if someone's on a cabinet, you can watch
             them play it rather than being locked out of the room.

Yours alone: money, tickets, prizes, inventory, and everything you win.
             Watching a friend hit a jackpot does not pay you, and it does
             not pay them twice.


VISITING SOMEONE ELSE'S ISLAND
------------------------------

This is the part worth understanding.

When you join someone, you're a guest on their island, so you see their
unlocks - their doors are open, their areas are available. When you leave,
your own progression comes back exactly as it was, and anything you
unlocked yourself while visiting stays yours.

The one gap: if the game crashes while you're a guest, that tidy-up never
runs. That's what the backup is for.


YOUR SAVE
---------

The first time you host or join in a play session, your save folder is
copied aside automatically. Five copies are kept. You'll find them in:

  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\devotid\...\TcgMultiplayer_SaveBackups\

The panel shows where the most recent one went, and has a button to make
one whenever you like.

This is real insurance, not a formality. If something ever goes wrong,
close the game, copy the files from the newest backup folder back over the
save, and you're where you were.


ACHIEVEMENTS AND LEADERBOARDS
-----------------------------

While you're in a session, the mod blocks the game from submitting anything
to Steam. This is deliberate: spectating replays your friend's round on your
machine, and without the block their score would post under your name.

Playing alone, with nobody connected, is completely untouched. Achievements
and leaderboards work exactly as they always have.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Stuck in a machine and can't get out?   Press F11.

Nothing happens when you press F9?      The mod didn't load. Open
                                        MelonLoader\Latest.log in the game
                                        folder - the answer is in there,
                                        usually near the bottom. Nine times
                                        out of ten it's the nested Mods
                                        folder from step 3.

Anything else - open the panel with F9. If part of the mod has switched
itself off, it says so at the top, in words, with a button to try again.

For a bug report, please send BOTH of these:

  <game folder>\TcgMultiplayer_Reports\session_<date>_host.txt   (or _guest)
  <game folder>\MelonLoader\Latest.log

The session report is written automatically every time you finish a session,
and there's a "Copy report" button for it in the panel. It says how long you
played, how the session ended, everyone's ping, and whether anything went
wrong. Nothing is sent anywhere - it's a file on your disk you choose to send.

Please say what you were doing, whether you were hosting or joining, and
what both of you saw - a lot of multiplayer bugs only make sense from both
sides.


UPDATING
--------

Delete Mods\TcgMultiplayer.dll and drop the new one in its place. You do not
need to touch MelonLoader again.


UNINSTALLING
------------

Delete Mods\TcgMultiplayer.dll. That removes the mod and leaves MelonLoader
and anything else you run alone. Your save is not touched.

To remove MelonLoader as well, run MelonLoader.Installer.exe again and click
UNINSTALL. Or let Steam do the whole job: right-click the game, Properties,
Installed Files, Verify integrity of game files.


ANTIVIRUS
---------

MelonLoader works by placing a file called version.dll next to the game.
It's a standard, widely used modding technique, but some antivirus software
is suspicious of it. If yours quarantines that file, the game simply starts
unmodded - nothing is damaged. You'd need to allow it for the mod to load.
