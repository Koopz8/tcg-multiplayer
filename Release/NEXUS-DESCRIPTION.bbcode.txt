[size=5][b]Play The Coin Game with a friend[/b][/size]

You share an island. You walk around it together, you take turns on the machines, and you watch each other play. Your money stays yours.

This is an unofficial fan mod. You each need your own copy of the game.

[b]This is a public beta.[/b] It has been tested heavily by one person and only lightly with two. Please read the "Your save" section below before you install.

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Two to four players over Steam's own networking. No servers, no port forwarding, no IP addresses. You host, hit Invite Friend, they join from the friends list.
[*]You see each other move around properly, with real animation, not dots on a map.
[*]Machines have an owner. If someone is on a cabinet it won't take your card, but you can stand there and watch them play it, coins and all, instead of being locked out of the room.
[*]Everyone keeps their own money, tickets, prizes and inventory. Watching a friend win a jackpot doesn't pay you, and doesn't pay them twice.
[*]Unlocks, doors and vehicles are shared while you play together, so the island opens up for everyone.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installing[/b][/size]
Manual install. Three steps, about ten minutes the first time and thirty seconds every time after that.

[b]1 - Find your game folder.[/b]
In Steam, right-click The Coin Game, then Manage, then Browse local files. That is the folder with TheCoinGame.exe in it. Leave the window open, you need it twice.

[b]2 - Install MelonLoader.[/b]
MelonLoader is what lets mods run at all. It is not made by me. Get MelonLoader.Installer.exe from the official site, [url=https://melonloader.net]melonloader.net[/url].

Run it, click SELECT and point it at TheCoinGame.exe. Pick [b]v0.6.6[/b] from the version list - not "Latest". Tick [b]x64[/b]. Leave everything else alone and click INSTALL.

Now launch the game once from Steam and let it reach the main menu. The first start is slow: MelonLoader is building its cache and can sit on a black screen for a minute or two. That is normal and only happens once. Quit when you see the menu.

[b]3 - Drop the mod in.[/b]
Open the zip. Inside is a folder called [b]Mods[/b]. Drag it into your game folder from step 1 and let Windows merge it. When you are done you should have:

[code]<game folder>\Mods\TcgMultiplayer.dll[/code]

If you end up with a Mods folder inside another Mods folder, drag it one level up. That is the only thing that usually goes wrong here.

Launch from Steam and press [b]F9[/b]. If the panel opens, you are done.

[b]Updating later:[/b] replace TcgMultiplayer.dll with the new one. [b]Uninstalling:[/b] delete it. MelonLoader stays put either way, and your save is never touched.

Everyone playing installs it, and everyone needs the same version. The mod checks, and will tell you if you don't match.

[i]Note: the other Coin Game mods here use BepInEx, and this one uses MelonLoader. Two mod loaders in one game folder often means the game starts with no mods at all and no hint why. Pick one.[/i]

[size=4][b]Your save[/b][/size]
Your save folder is copied aside automatically the first time you host or join. Five copies are kept, in a TcgMultiplayer_SaveBackups folder next to your save.

When you visit someone else's island you see their unlocks while you are there, and your own progression is put back when you leave - keeping anything you unlocked yourself. The one gap is a crash mid-session, which is what the backup is for.

Press [b]F10[/b] any time to run a built-in self-check. It verifies, against your own save, that a full visit gives your progression back. It takes a couple of seconds.

[size=4][b]Achievements and leaderboards[/b][/size]
While you are in a session, the mod blocks the game from submitting anything to Steam.

This is deliberate. Spectating works by replaying your friend's round on your machine - without the block, their score would post to the leaderboard under your name. I was not willing to ship something that quietly pollutes the leaderboards.

[b]Playing alone, with nobody connected, is completely untouched.[/b] Achievements and leaderboards work exactly as they always have.

[size=4][b]Controls[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]F9[/b] - open the multiplayer panel
[*][b]F10[/b] - run the self-check
[*][b]F11[/b] - get unstuck, releases every machine you are holding
[/list]
All keys and settings are in MelonLoader's config, under TcgMultiplayer.

[size=4][b]Known issues and limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Two to four players. Four is what the bandwidth is designed around.
[*]No host migration. If the host leaves, the session ends for everyone. It ends cleanly and everyone keeps their own progression, but it ends.
[*]NPCs are not synced. You will each see them doing their own thing.
[*]Both players need the same version of the game. A Coin Game update will probably need a mod update.
[*]Voice chat is not in. Use Discord.
[*]This is a beta. The stranger corners of the game have not been tested with two people. Please report what you find.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Bug reports[/b][/size]
The mod writes a session report every time you finish playing. Please send it along with your MelonLoader log. Both live in the game folder - Steam, right-click The Coin Game, Manage, Browse local files:
[list]
[*]TcgMultiplayer_Reports\session_....txt
[*]MelonLoader\Latest.log
[/list]
If you can, send both files from [b]both[/b] players. Half a multiplayer bug usually is not enough to fix it.

Nothing is sent anywhere on its own. The session report is a plain text file on your disk that you choose to send.
