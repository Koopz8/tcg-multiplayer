[size=5][b]The Coin Game, but with your friends[/b][/size]

2-4 players on one island over steam. you can see each other walk around, watch each other play the machines, ride in each others cars. everyone keeps their own money.

unofficial fan mod, nothing to do with devotid. everyone needs their own copy of the game.

[b]public beta.[/b] it works, but it's a beta - read the "your save" bit before installing.

[size=4][b]what it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]host makes a friends-only steam lobby, friends join from the friends list. no servers, no port forwarding.
[*]you see each other properly, walking animations and all.
[*]machines have an owner. if someone's on a cabinet it won't take your card, but you can stand there and watch them play it - the coins, the claw and their score on the cabinet screen, live.
[*]tickets and prizes a friend wins show up on the floor for everyone. only they can pick them up.
[*]everyone keeps their own money, tickets, prizes and inventory. watching a friend win doesn't pay you.
[*]unlocks, doors and vehicles are shared while you play together.
[*]vehicles actually drive for everyone. ride along in a car a friend is driving (F7).
[*]monitor picker - the game has no setting for which screen it opens on, so the F9 panel has one. pick a monitor, tell it to remember, done. F8 cycles.
[/list]

[size=4][b]installing[/b][/size]
manual install, takes about 10 min the first time.

[b]1.[/b] find your game folder. steam > right click The Coin Game > Manage > Browse local files.

[b]2.[/b] install MelonLoader from [url=https://melonloader.net]melonloader.net[/url] (not made by me). run the installer, point it at TheCoinGame.exe, pick [b]v0.6.6[/b] from the list (not "latest"), tick x64, install. launch the game once and let it reach the main menu - first launch is slow, it's building a cache. quit.

[b]3.[/b] open the zip, drag the [b]Mods[/b] folder into your game folder. you should end up with:
[code]<game folder>\Mods\TcgMultiplayer.dll[/code]
if you get a Mods folder inside a Mods folder, drag it up one. that's the only thing that ever goes wrong.

launch, press [b]F9[/b]. if the panel opens you're good.

updating = replace the dll. uninstalling = delete it. everyone playing needs the same version, the mod checks.

[i]heads up: the other coin game mods use BepInEx, this one uses MelonLoader. both in one folder = the game starts with no mods and no error. pick one.[/i]

[size=4][b]your save[/b][/size]
your save gets copied aside the first time you host or join (5 copies kept, in TcgMultiplayer_SaveBackups next to your save). when you visit someone's island you see their unlocks, and your own get put back when you leave. F10 runs a self check against your real save if you want to see it work.

[size=4][b]achievements[/b][/size]
blocked while you're in a session, on purpose. watching a friend's round replays it on your machine, so without this their skee ball score could post under your name. playing alone is untouched.

[size=4][b]keys[/b][/size]
F9 panel · F7 ride along · F8 move game to next monitor · F10 self check · F11 unstick everything

[size=4][b]known stuff[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]no host migration. host leaves, session ends (cleanly, nobody loses anything).
[*]npcs aren't synced.
[*]you see other players as your own character for now (if you're mandy and they're larry, they look like mandy to you). next on the list.
[*]a coin game update will probably break it until I update.
[*]no voice chat, use discord.
[*]beta. weird corners of the game haven't been tried with two people. tell me what you find.
[/list]

[size=4][b]bugs[/b][/size]
tick [b]Tester mode[/b] at the bottom of the F9 panel before you play - it puts the numbers in the report. the mod writes a report file after every session in TcgMultiplayer_Reports next to the game. send me that plus MelonLoader\Latest.log, from both players if you can. nothing is uploaded automatically.
